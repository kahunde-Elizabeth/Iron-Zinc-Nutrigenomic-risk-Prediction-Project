# Ethics Statement

- This project uses only publicly available, de-identified data from the 1000 Genomes Project.
- No primary data collection involving human participants is performed.
- No attempt will be made to re-identify any individual.
- All results are for educational and research purposes only.
- No clinical recommendations or medical advice will be generated.
- The work aligns with the principles of the Uganda National Council for Science and Technology (UNCST) regarding secondary analysis of open genomic data.
